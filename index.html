Our story

Familiar flavours,
unhurried hospitality.

Epicuria began with a simple idea: the food we grew up with deserves the same care, produce and theatre as the world's great dining rooms. Our kitchen moves between Indian regional classics, Italian comfort food and the street-food flavours of Asia — always cooked to order.

Whether it's a weeknight dinner for two or a full festive table, the room is yours for as long as you need it.
