Home/Occasions

Celebrate with us

Some evenings deserve more than dinner. For festivals, birthdays and the days in between, we build menus around the table — and the people around it.

Festive tables

A menu for the moment.

Each festive menu is a starting point. Tell us your group size, dietary needs and budget, and we will tailor the rest. Menus are available on request and subject to seasonal produce.

Diwali

Celebrate the festival of lights with a table built for sharing.

  • Starters — Paneer tikka, tandoori mushrooms, samosas
  • Mains — Dal makhani, shahi paneer, butter chicken, Hyderabadi biryani
  • Desserts — Gulab jamun, kaju katli, rasmalai
  • Drinks — Masala chai, cold coffee

Holi

A rainbow of flavours for the festival of colours.

  • Starters — Colourful chaat platters, veg seekh kebab, chicken tikka
  • Mains — Kachori, veg korma, rogan josh
  • Desserts — Gujiya, malpua
  • Drinks — Thandai, lassi, kesar milk

Eid

Rich delicacies and a slow afternoon with family.

  • Starters — Chicken seekh kebab, shammi kebab
  • Mains — Mutton biryani, chicken korma, nihari
  • Desserts — Sheer khurma, phirni, seviyan kheer
  • Drinks — Roohafza sherbet, cold coffee

Navratri

Pure, satvik fasting food — cooked with care, served with respect.

  • Starters — Sabudana vada, fruit chaat
  • Mains — Kuttu ki puri, vrat ke aloo, sama ka chawal
  • Desserts — Makhana kheer, coconut ladoo, kaju barfi
  • Drinks — Coconut water, mango lassi

Independence Day

Celebrate freedom with every bite — in saffron, white and green.

  • Starters — Tricolour dhokla, vegetable kebab
  • Mains — Tiranga pulao, paneer tikka masala, butter naan
  • Desserts — Tricolour barfi, kesari rasgulla
  • Drinks — Tiranga lassi, Indian flag punch
